虞圣呈博士现为慕尼黑工业大学计算、信息与技术学院软件工程与人工智能教席博士后研究员，与陈春阳教授合作，并为南京大学智能软件工程实验室成员。他于2020年和2024年分别获南京大学软件学院软件工程学士和博士学位，博士阶段由陈振宇教授和房春荣副教授指导，博士论文聚焦知识增强的智能GUI测试。2022至2023年，他赴苏黎世联邦理工学院计算机系访学并由苏振东教授指导。

其研究围绕智能体驱动的软件质量保障和面向智能体质量保障的软件工程两条主线展开，以软件交互为研究对象，以智能化为技术目标，以安全为研究约束，并以可信为总体目标。具体方向包括智能体集成软件的安全性与可控性、交互行为验证与运行时保障、智能体集成软件质量工程、软件交互质量保障、意图驱动的智能任务执行，以及智能体集成软件中的人-智能体协作。

他主持DFG项目1项，共同主持ACM SIGSOFT暑期/冬季学校资助项目1项，并作为骨干或参与成员参与国家重点研发计划和国家自然科学基金项目。他已在CSUR、TOSEM、TSE、ICSE、FSE等软件工程顶尖期刊和会议发表论文40余篇，其中20余篇由其担任第一作者、共同第一作者或通讯作者。围绕上述研究方向，他研发了若干工具并发表系列工业与工具论文，相关成果已在英飞凌科技、国家电网、北汽、中国联通、华为鸿蒙和广东软件园等单位得到深入应用。

他曾获ASE 2026 Distinguished Reviewer Award、南京大学优秀博士学位论文奖、国家奖学金、南京大学栋梁奖学金学术优秀奖、南京大学-HPI研究奖学金、南京大学博士生校长特别奖学金等荣誉。他曾担任ICSE、FSE、ASE、ISSTA、IJCAI、ICSME、IUI、MSR等软件工程、人工智能和人机交互领域会议程序委员会委员，并担任TOSEM、TSE、TDSC、JSS、IST、EMSE、IEEE TRel、IEEE TIV等期刊审稿人。他曾受邀在Oxford Internet Institute、fortiss和IPAI作学术报告，现于慕尼黑工业大学主讲自动化移动应用测试研讨课。

Dr. Shengcheng Yu is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair of Software Engineering & AI, School of Computation, Information and Technology, Technical University of Munich, where he works with Prof. Chunyang Chen. He is also a member of the Intelligent Software Engineering Laboratory at Nanjing University. He received his bachelor’s and Ph.D. degrees in Software Engineering from the Software Institute, Nanjing University, in 2020 and 2024, respectively. His doctoral research on knowledge-enhanced intelligent GUI testing was supervised by Prof. Zhenyu Chen and Assoc. Prof. Chunrong Fang. From 2022 to 2023, he was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at ETH Zurich under the supervision of Prof. Zhendong Su.

His research follows two lines: Agents for Software Quality and Software Engineering for Agent Quality. It treats software interaction as the research object, intelligence as the technical objective, security as a research constraint, and trustworthiness as the overarching goal. Specific directions include security and controllability of agent-integrated software, interaction verification and runtime assurance, quality engineering for agent-integrated software, software interaction quality assurance, intent-driven intelligent task execution, and human-agent collaboration in agent-integrated software.

He is the principal investigator of one DFG-funded project and co-principal investigator of one ACM SIGSOFT-funded Summer/Winter School project. He has also contributed as a key participant or project member to projects funded by the National Key R&D Program of China and the National Natural Science Foundation of China. He has published more than 40 papers in top-tier software engineering journals and conferences, including CSUR, TOSEM, TSE, ICSE, and FSE. He is the first, co-first, or corresponding author of more than 20 of these papers. His research has produced several tools and a series of industry and tool papers. The resulting technologies have been extensively adopted in practice by organizations including Infineon Technologies, State Grid Corporation of China, BAIC, China Unicom, Huawei’s HarmonyOS ecosystem, and Guangdong Software Park.

His honors include the ASE 2026 Distinguished Reviewer Award, the Nanjing University Outstanding Ph.D. Dissertation Award, the National Scholarship, the Nanjing University Dongliang Scholarship for Excellence in Academic Achievement, the Nanjing University-HPI Research Scholarship, and the Nanjing University President’s Special Scholarship for Ph.D. Students. He has served on the program committees of ICSE, FSE, ASE, ISSTA, IJCAI, ICSME, IUI, and MSR in software engineering, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction. He reviews for TOSEM, TSE, TDSC, JSS, IST, EMSE, IEEE TRel, and IEEE TIV. He has given invited talks at the Oxford Internet Institute, fortiss, and IPAI, and currently teaches the Seminar on Automated Mobile App Testing at the Technical University of Munich.
